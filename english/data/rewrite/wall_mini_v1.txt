The Great Wall of China (a very long wall in the north of China) is many old walls in one line.
They were built on the north side of old Chinese kingdoms (a country that a king rules) to stop people who came on horses from the north.
The first walls were made in the 7th century BC (before Christ), and later the Qin dynasty (the time when China was first one country) joined them together.
After that, every dynasty made the wall bigger. The parts we know best today were made by the Ming dynasty (1368-1644).

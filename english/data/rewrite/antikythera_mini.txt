The Antikythera mechanism (a very old machine from Greece) is a machine that shows the movement of the sky. People made it by hand in the 2nd century BC (before Christ) and people found it again in 1901.
It is the oldest computer we know that works like a computer today, but with wheels and gears (round things that go around and around).
With it, people could tell where the stars and the sun would be in the sky many years later.
It could also show the 4-year cycle of the Olympic Games (a big sport event in Greece).
